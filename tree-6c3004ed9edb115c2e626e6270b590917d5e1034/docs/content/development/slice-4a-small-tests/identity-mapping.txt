Method
------
Per crate, before the move:
  cargo nextest list --locked -p <pkg>    (from the repo root)
plus the workspace enumeration:
  cargo nextest list --locked --workspace
Outputs were byte-sorted for diffing (list-*.txt in this directory) and
re-run after the move. Every line is one test identity
("<binary> <module>::<test>"). Zero identities were lost: per-crate counts
are equal before/after and the workspace total is unchanged.
